## Brand & Style
The brand personality is calm, reassuring, transparent, and approachable. It speaks to modern Indian retail investors who seek financial independence without overwhelming jargon, high-pressure urgency, or frenetic trading tickers. The emotional response is grounded optimism—imparting absolute security, steady long-term compounding, and quiet confidence.

The design movement blends **Minimalism** with subtle **Tactile Tonal Depth**. Large fields of warm, unbleached off-white allow crisp typography and generous spacing to breathe. Rather than aggressive gamification or flashing visual noise, interfaces employ measured, organic seed and origami-inspired structural motifs, matte finishes, precise borders, and focused touches of vivid lime to indicate active, forward progress.

## Colors
The palette balances institutional stability with vibrant, modern vitality:

- **Primary (`#176B52`)**: Deep Emerald Green acts as the anchor for primary actions, critical brand elements, high-emphasis icons, and structural focus rings.
- **Secondary / Accent (`#D5F36A`)**: Vivid Electric Lime functions strictly as a focal accent—used for interactive milestones, micro-badges, progress callouts, and key highlight states against deep green or dark charcoal backgrounds.
- **Tertiary / Soft Success (`#E8F0E9`)**: Pale Green Surface provides a restorative background for positive portfolio metrics, completed verification banners, and subtle interactive states.
- **Neutral Primary (`#202923`)**: Deep Slate Charcoal for primary body copy, titles, and high-contrast iconography.
- **Neutral Secondary (`#5F6B63`)**: Muted Green-Slate for supporting labels, meta-information, timestamps, and input placeholders.
- **Canvas & Surface**: Background canvas relies on `#F7F8F5` (Soft Off-White), layered with `#FFFFFF` for elevated structural cards, modules, and form containers.
- **Borders & Dividers**: Structured subtly with `#E4E9E3` to preserve separation without high visual tension.
- **Error & Destructive (`#B42318`)**: An accessible crimson red applied strictly to invalid input validation messages, critical security alerts, or irreversible destructive actions.
- **Disabled State**: `#E5E7EB` for inactive backgrounds with `#6B7280` for low-contrast text.

## Typography
The system is built entirely on **Inter** to ensure maximum legibility across Indian language rendering contexts, varying screen resolutions, and dense numerical datasets.

- **Numerics & Tabular Figures**: Financial amounts, returns percentages, and currency symbols (`₹`) must use open-type tabular figures (`tnum`) to maintain vertical alignment in data tables and portfolio summaries.
- **Rhythm & Line Height**: Body text adheres strictly to a 1.5 relative line height for relaxed reading, mitigating cognitive fatigue during onboarding and disclosure reviews.
- **Hierarchy Rules**: Section headings use `600` weight to separate hierarchy without the harshness of heavy bold blocks, while hero banners leverage `700` weight for definitive, confident statements.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid-responsive responsive model constrained within maximum content wrappers:

- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with `24px` outer screen margins and `16px` gutters. Target viewport is baseline 360px. Tap zones preserve a minimum 48px height.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8-column layout with `32px` margins and `20px` gutters.
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px)**: 12-column layout fixed at a maximum container width of `1200px`, centered with auto-margins, featuring `24px` gutters.

Spacing scales on a consistent 4px/8px mathematical base. Component padding utilizes `space-md` (16px) and `space-lg` (24px) for generous internal breathing room, reinforcing an uncluttered, premium investment experience.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation eschews dramatic, muddy drop shadows in favor of **low-contrast outlines** paired with **ambient tinted diffusion**:

- **Layer 0 (Base)**: Soft off-white canvas (`#F7F8F5`).
- **Layer 1 (Card/Container)**: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with a `1px` continuous border in `#E4E9E3`. Ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(32, 41, 35, 0.04), 0 4px 12px rgba(32, 41, 35, 0.02)`.
- **Layer 2 (Floating Modals, Dropdowns & Popovers)**: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) surface with subtle border `#E4E9E3` and deep ambient shadow: `0 8px 24px rgba(23, 107, 82, 0.08), 0 2px 6px rgba(32, 41, 35, 0.04)`.
- **Focus Depth**: High-visibility focus indicators utilize an offset: `0 0 0 2px #FFFFFF, 0 0 0 4px #176B52`.

## Shapes
The design uses a balanced 12px structural corner radius (`roundedness: 2`, where default base elements resolve to 12px / `0.75rem`), creating an approachable yet structurally sound shape vocabulary:

- **Cards, Modules & Sheets**: Unified at `12px` corner radius.
- **Inputs & Interactive Controls**: `12px` corner radius to match cards and foster physical consistency.
- **Pills & Micro-Tags**: Full rounded pills (`9999px`) are reserved strictly for status chips, stock categories, and contextual filters.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Deep Emerald Green (`#176B52`) background, `#FFFFFF` text, `12px` border radius, minimum height of `48px` (desktop) and `56px` (mobile sticky action). Hover state tints to `#125641`. Active state compresses slightly (`scale: 0.99`).
- **Secondary**: `#FFFFFF` background with `1px` border in `#176B52`, text in `#176B52`.
- **Accent Action**: Accent Lime (`#D5F36A`) background with `#202923` charcoal text for celebratory deposit actions and onboarding triggers.
- **Disabled**: `#E5E7EB` background with `#6B7280` text, zero elevation, cursor not-allowed.

### Form Inputs
- **Height**: Fixed at `56px` for effortless thumb tap accuracy.
- **Surface**: `#FFFFFF` background with `1px` border in `#E4E9E3` and `12px` border radius.
- **Typography**: Label floats or rests cleanly above input using `label-md` (`#5F6B63`). Input text set in `body-lg` (`#202923`).
- **Active / Focus**: `1px` border changes to `#176B52` backed by a `2px` focus halo (`rgba(23, 107, 82, 0.15)`).
- **Error State**: `1px` solid `#B42318` border with caption text in `#B42318` paired with an alert icon.

### Cards
- **Standard Card**: White `#FFFFFF` surface, `12px` corner radius, `1px` border in `#E4E9E3`, padded with `24px` (`space-lg`).
- **Highlighted Metric Card**: Tertiary surface `#E8F0E9` with zero border, displaying portfolio returns with deep green figures and subtle origami-seed watermarks.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips**: Pill-shaped (`9999px`), `4px` vertical padding, `12px` horizontal padding.
- **Success Chip**: `#E8F0E9` background with `#176B52` text.
- **Accent Chip**: `#D5F36A` background with `#202923` text for special yield highlights or "New" indicators.

### Lists
- Borderless list rows with `16px` vertical padding, demarcated by `#E4E9E3` 1px divider lines inset to content start. Trailing values aligned with `tnum` tabular numerics.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size: `20px × 20px` with `4px` radius for checkboxes; full circle for radio buttons.
- Inactive: `#FFFFFF` fill with `1.5px` border in `#5F6B63`.
- Selected: Solid `#176B52` fill with white checkmark/dot indicator.

### Additional Specialty Components
- **SIP Calculator Module**: Smooth slider track with `#176B52` active fill, `#D5F36A` accent handle, dynamic returns projection breakdown cards.
- **KYC Stepper**: Horizontal node indicators with animated check transforms, utilizing pale green `#E8F0E9` completed paths and emerald active rings.
